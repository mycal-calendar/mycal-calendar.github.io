Privacy Policy

Calendar access

MyCal accesses iOS Calendar data through Apple’s EventKit framework. With your permission, it processes calendar names, account/source information, colours, event titles, start/end dates, all-day status and event/occurrence identifiers to provide calendar views, search and widgets. Apple’s EventKitUI detail and editing screens may display additional event information.

When you initiate and confirm an event creation, edit or deletion, that action is applied to the underlying calendar event. MyCal-specific account, calendar and event colour preferences are local; they do not change the original calendar colour in Apple Calendar.

Gmail, Outlook, iCloud and other provider calendars are available when configured in iOS Calendar and accessible through EventKit. MyCal does not provide a separate sign-in flow or operate its own calendar synchronisation server for those accounts. Apple’s and your calendar provider’s synchronisation and privacy practices apply separately.

Local storage and widget sharing

Language, themes, appearance, calendar selections and custom colour preferences are stored in the app’s local area. Colour choices are associated with local identifiers and occurrence keys.

The app and MyCal widget extension use a shared file area restricted to components in the same App Group. Widgets do not automatically inherit calendar permission; the app writes calendar and event summaries into this area. Summaries may contain event titles, times, calendar/source identifiers, names, colours and appearance preferences.

Because each widget can select different calendars, the shared cache may contain a broader calendar catalogue and date range than the app’s currently visible filter. Hiding a calendar does not revoke iOS calendar access or necessarily remove all cached data.

People viewing your Home Screen may see event information displayed by widgets. Choose widget contents and device lock/preview settings accordingly. Widget content may remain outdated temporarily because iOS controls refresh timing.

Data leaving the device

The reviewed source version did not reveal a developer-operated data-upload service, advertising network or analytics SDK. This finding is not a blanket promise that no information is collected under any circumstances, including operating-system services, Apple Calendar synchronisation, device backups, App Store/TestFlight diagnostics or information you choose to send to support.

In-app timing measurements evaluate elapsed time between calendar queries and interface operations. No flow sending those measurements to a developer server was identified in this version. Changes to the app or its data flows require a new review of this policy and the store privacy disclosures.

Permissions and choices

You can change or revoke calendar access in iOS Settings. This does not delete your Apple Calendar events. Widget updates following revocation may depend on the app’s and iOS’s next refresh; immediate erasure is not promised. Custom colours and hidden calendars can be managed in MyCal.

The source includes a contacts permission description for displaying participant names. No direct address-book query or export was identified in MyCal’s reviewed code; Apple’s system detail and editing interfaces are subject to their own permission behaviour.

Retention, deletion and support

Local preferences and caches are retained for app functionality and updated as relevant data or preferences change. A fixed automatic retention period or a one-step erase-all-data function was not verified in this version. App removal and backup management follow iOS behaviour; immediate removal of backup copies is not promised. Uninstalling MyCal does not automatically delete Apple Calendar events.

For support, contact mycal.app.support@gmail.com. You do not need to send private event titles, account addresses or sensitive screenshots when requesting help. Email correspondence is a separate data flow from on-device calendar processing; do not include information that is not needed for your request.

About this website

This static site has no custom analytics, contact form, external fonts or embedded media. If published on GitHub Pages, GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. Hosting is a separate data flow from the MyCal app.
