Getting started & common questions

How do I connect my calendars?

MyCal uses iOS Calendar through EventKit. Configure your calendar account in iOS Settings and allow MyCal calendar access. Availability depends on what the account exposes to iOS Calendar.

How do I add the widgets?

Touch and hold an empty area of your Home Screen, open the widget gallery and search for MyCal. Add the monthly calendar or daily agenda. Menu wording can vary by iOS version.

Can each widget show different calendars?

Yes. Touch and hold a MyCal widget and choose Edit Widget. Select its calendars and appearance independently, or use the app’s calendar selection.

Why is a widget not up to date?

iOS controls refresh timing. Open MyCal to allow it to refresh its shared calendar summary. Updates may still be delayed by the system; instant updates are not guaranteed.

What happens when I change a colour?

MyCal-specific colours are local preferences. They do not change the original calendar colour. Event colour takes priority over calendar colour, then account colour, then the original calendar colour.

Will my preferences transfer to another phone?

Automatic transfer of MyCal preferences between devices is not promised. Your calendar provider’s synchronisation is separate from MyCal’s local colour and appearance settings.

How do I manage calendar permission?

Use iOS Settings to change MyCal’s calendar access. Revoking access does not delete your calendar events. A widget may need a later app or system refresh before its display reflects the change.

A safer support request

For a support request, include only what is needed:

  • MyCal version and iOS version
  • Which view or widget is affected
  • Steps to reproduce the problem
  • Expected and observed behaviour

Do not send private event titles, calendar exports, account passwords or unredacted screenshots.
